About Us

Connecting entrepreneurs with Toronto’s startup ecosystem.

We help founders access the programs, people, spaces, funding resources, and knowledge they need to build successful companies.

Our Mission

Startup Here Toronto exists to make entrepreneurship more accessible. Whether you are starting with an idea or scaling a company, we help you find the right support at the right time.

We connect founders with community partners, public programs, workshops, startup events, funding opportunities, and innovation resources.

We support:

  • Early-stage founders
  • Student entrepreneurs
  • Tech startups
  • Small business innovators
  • Scaleup companies
Our Values

Built around access, connection, and growth.

Inclusive Innovation

We believe entrepreneurship should be accessible to founders from every background.

Community First

Strong startups grow faster when founders are connected to the right people.

Practical Support

We focus on useful resources that help founders make real business progress.
